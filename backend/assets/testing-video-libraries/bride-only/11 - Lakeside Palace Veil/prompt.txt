PROJECT: LADIES PLANET — PREMIUM BRIDAL VIDEO TRANSFORMATION

TASK:
Preserve the original reference video's performance, motion,
camera movements, transitions, timing, and environment while
replacing the bride's identity and dress and ensuring a luxurious,
ornament-rich bridal appearance.

This is a reference-video transformation task.

Do not create a new wedding scene.
Do not invent an opening sequence.
Do not invent actions that do not exist in the reference video.

==================================================
1. REFERENCE INPUTS
==================================================

REFERENCE VIDEO:
The master source for the original scene, actions, motion,
camera movement, timing, composition, transitions, lighting,
background, and visual effects.

BRIDE REFERENCE IMAGE:
The source for the replacement bride's facial identity,
recognizable features, skin tone, and natural appearance.

DRESS REFERENCE IMAGE:
The source for the replacement bridal dress, including its
design, silhouette, color, embroidery, fabric, and embellishments.

Use the supplied references dynamically.

Do not hardcode a particular bride's name, facial description,
dress color, or dress design.

==================================================
2. ABSOLUTE RULE: DO NOT INVENT NEW SCENES
==================================================

The reference video is the master for the entire sequence,
including its very first frame.

Start with the same type of scene, subject position, environment,
and action shown at the beginning of the reference video.

Do not invent a new opening.

Do not make the bride emerge from water.
Do not make the bride rise out of a pool, lake, river, ocean,
or any other body of water unless that action explicitly occurs
in the original reference video.

Do not introduce water, underwater environments, fountains,
waterfalls, beaches, swimming pools, or water-based transitions
that are absent from the reference.

Do not make the bride appear suddenly from a new location.

Do not make the bride materialize, float into the scene,
descend from the sky, emerge from smoke, or appear through
an artificial magical effect.

Do not introduce a runway entrance, staircase entrance,
walking sequence, or dramatic reveal unless it is present
in the original reference video.

Do not invent any other opening action.

The original video determines what happens at the beginning.

If the original video begins with the bride already standing,
sitting, walking, turning, dancing, or interacting with another
person, preserve that original starting situation.

Do not replace the original opening with a new cinematic idea.

==================================================
3. PRESERVE THE ORIGINAL PERFORMANCE
==================================================

Use the reference video as the master motion source.

Preserve the original bride's visible performance as closely
as the model allows.

Maintain the original:

- Body position.
- Body orientation.
- Head movement.
- Head turns.
- Facial expression timing.
- Gaze direction.
- Hand and arm movements.
- Walking and turning movements.
- Poses and posture changes.
- Interaction with the groom and other people.
- Movement speed.
- Slow-motion timing.
- Overall action progression.

Do not invent new gestures.

Do not add actions merely to make the result more dramatic.

Do not remove important actions from the original performance.

The replacement bride must follow the same movement trajectory
and temporal progression as the original bride.

Preserve the original performance rather than generating
a new performance from scratch.

==================================================
4. PRESERVE THE ORIGINAL CAMERA AND EDITING
==================================================

Treat the reference video as the authoritative source for
cinematography and editing.

Preserve its:

- Camera angles.
- Camera height.
- Framing.
- Subject distance.
- Camera movement direction.
- Camera movement speed.
- Tracking and panning.
- Push-ins and pull-outs.
- Zooms, if present.
- Original shot sequence.
- Shot duration.
- Transition timing.
- Slow-motion sections.
- Motion blur.
- Lighting direction.
- Color grading.
- Existing visual effects.

Do not introduce camera movements absent from the reference.

Do not add arbitrary close-ups, drone shots, orbiting shots,
or unrelated cinematic transitions.

Do not change the original video into a fashion runway montage.

Maintain the original temporal structure and visual continuity
as closely as the model permits.

==================================================
5. BRIDE IDENTITY REPLACEMENT
==================================================

Replace the original bride's facial identity with the identity
shown in BRIDE_REFERENCE.

Preserve the supplied bride's recognizable facial features,
face shape, eye shape, nose, lips, skin tone, and proportions.

Maintain the same identity throughout the video.

Adapt the new face naturally to the original head movement,
viewing angles, expressions, lighting, and motion blur.

Do not freeze the face into a static expression.

Do not allow the face to flicker, warp, or change identity
between frames.

Do not generate a different bride.

Do not modify the groom's identity or appearance.

==================================================
6. DRESS REPLACEMENT
==================================================

Replace the original bridal dress with the garment shown
in DRESS_REFERENCE.

The supplied dress image is the authoritative garment source.

Preserve its actual:

- Color.
- Silhouette.
- Neckline.
- Sleeves.
- Bodice construction.
- Waistline.
- Skirt design.
- Length.
- Embroidery.
- Decorative patterns.
- Beadwork.
- Fabric texture.
- Distinctive embellishments.

Do not copy the original bride's dress.

Do not combine the original dress with the replacement dress.

Do not invent a different garment.

Adapt the replacement dress to the original bride's body pose
and movement without changing the original performance.

Maintain realistic fabric physics, natural folds, appropriate
weight, and believable movement.

The dress must remain visually consistent throughout the video.

==================================================
7. LUXURIOUS, ORNAMENT-RICH BRIDAL APPEARANCE
==================================================

The replacement bride must have a premium, luxurious,
ornament-rich bridal appearance suitable for a high-end
Indian bridal boutique.

Jewellery is an important part of the final bridal styling.

Create an elegant, richly ornamented bridal look with suitable
traditional Indian bridal jewellery.

Depending on the original video framing and the bride's styling,
include appropriate pieces such as:

- An elaborate bridal necklace or layered necklaces.
- Matching earrings, including traditional jhumkas where suitable.
- A maang tikka or other appropriate forehead ornament.
- A coordinated headpiece if suitable for the bride's hairstyle.
- Elegant bangles or bridal bracelets.
- A matching ring or other appropriate bridal accessory.
- A traditional waist belt or kamarband only if appropriate
  for the supplied dress and overall styling.

Use coordinated jewellery that complements the supplied dress
and the bride's appearance.

The overall styling should feel luxurious, carefully selected,
photorealistic, and suitable for a premium bridal campaign.

JEWELLERY QUALITY:

The ornaments should appear physically real, with believable
metal surfaces, intricate detailing, gemstones, pearls, and
natural reflections where appropriate.

Jewellery should have convincing weight and natural movement.

Necklaces must follow the neckline and contours of the body.

Earrings must remain attached naturally to the ears.

Forehead ornaments must remain aligned with the forehead
and hairline.

Bangles must follow the wrist and hand movements.

Avoid floating jewellery, fused ornaments, distorted chains,
impossible connections, excessive sparkle effects, and
flickering details.

JEWELLERY CONSISTENCY:

Maintain the same jewellery design, placement, and overall
appearance throughout the video.

Do not randomly change the necklace, earrings, headpiece,
or bangles between frames.

Do not generate a different jewellery set every time the
camera angle changes.

Do not allow jewellery to disappear and reappear.

Do not allow ornaments to penetrate the bride's skin or dress.

JEWELLERY AND DRESS COMPATIBILITY:

Coordinate the jewellery with the supplied dress.

Choose complementary tones, materials, and ornament styles.

Do not change the actual dress design to accommodate jewellery.

Do not allow jewellery to cover important dress details
unnecessarily.

The objective is a rich bridal appearance, not excessive,
uncoordinated ornamentation.

IMPORTANT:

The original video's movement and camera framing must remain
unchanged merely to show the jewellery.

Do not introduce new jewellery close-ups or additional shots
that are absent from the reference.
