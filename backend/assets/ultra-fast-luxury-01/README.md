The owner-supplied prompt is preserved in prompt.txt without rewriting.
Model: Higgsfield Grok Imagine 1.5 (grok_video_v15).
Output: 10 seconds, landscape 16:9 by default.

The source supplied by the owner is the Downloads video beginning
"A vision of grace, love, and endless beauty". Its first ten seconds of
audio were extracted locally to music.m4a. The thumbnail and scene review
were also extracted locally from this source. No AI generation was used.

Only bride, dress-view and jewellery image references go to Grok.
The source video and music are NOT sent as generation references.
The existing finishVideo pipeline adds music.m4a to the returned video
before exposing the final output on the platform. This uses the original
soundtrack; it does not create new singing, speech or music.

This template is owner-supplied, not generation-tested or marked passed.
